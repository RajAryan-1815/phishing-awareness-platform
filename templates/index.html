⚠️ What is Phishing?

Phishing is a type of cyber attack where criminals impersonate trusted organisations (banks, tech companies, government agencies) to trick you into revealing sensitive information such as passwords, credit card numbers or personal details.

Attacks arrive via email, SMS, phone calls or fake websites. They often use urgency, fear or tempting offers to bypass your critical thinking.

The best defence is awareness. This platform walks you through real-world techniques so you can spot and avoid them.
